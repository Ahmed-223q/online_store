Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options?

We offer free shipping on orders over $999. Standard shipping is $30. International orders ship within 5–10 business days. Express options are available at checkout.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be unworn, in original packaging with all tags attached. Contact our support team to initiate a return.

Are all products 100% authentic?

Absolutely. VALUE Store is an authorized retailer of all brands we carry. Every product comes with an authenticity certificate and brand packaging.

How do promo codes work?

Enter your promo code at checkout in the "Promo Code" field. Try VALUE10 for 10% off, LUXURY20 for 20% off, or SAVE50 for $50 off your order.
